# Problem segmentation tree (draft)

Macro: people live in ageing buildings and cannot judge whether visible cracks are dangerous.

- Residents cannot read cracks
  - They cannot tell harmless from structural cracks
  - A wrong all-clear lands on them
- Engineers are too few
  - No ranked list of which building to visit first
  - Reports queue up (Tripoli: reported to a call centre, not inspected)
- Photos are hard to interpret
  - Quality (blur, light), scale (width needs a reference), element and material vary
- Data is scarce
  - Public data is mostly concrete and pavement, with few columns, beams or brick walls
  - No engineer-labelled phone photos yet

Atomic (to work on first): read one photo of one crack on one element and say Low, Medium, High or "Expert needed", with the reason.
